Data Engineering Intern

Remote, United States · Internship, 12 weeks · Summer 2027

Apply now

About the role

Kestrel Grid builds forecasting software that regional power grids use to plan for tomorrow's demand. As a data engineering intern you'll join the platform team that moves meter, weather, and market data into the models our operators rely on every morning.

What you'll do

  • Build and test batch pipelines in Python and SQL that load hourly meter readings.
  • Add data quality checks to our Airflow jobs and fix the gaps they find.
  • Pair with a forecasting engineer on one feature that ships before the end of summer.

Qualifications

  • Working toward a degree in computer science, data science, or a related field.
  • Experience with Python and SQL from coursework or your own projects.
  • Familiarity with Git and the command line.

Compensation and benefits

Pay range: $32 to $38 per hour, plus a home office stipend and a mentor for the whole internship.
