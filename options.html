What it costs and what gets sent

  • You pay Anthropic directly. Each time you click Find friends, Job Notifier sends one request to Anthropic's API with your key. It's billed to your Anthropic account at Anthropic's prices. Job Notifier itself is free. Anthropic API pricing
  • Sent to Anthropic: the job posting text (up to 8,000 characters), and each included friend's name, skills, interests, and level.
  • Never sent: your friends' email addresses and any recruiter link you paste. They stay in this browser. Your API key goes only to Anthropic, to authorize each request. Your passphrase never leaves this browser.
  • Encrypted with your passphrase. Your API key and friends list are encrypted in this Chrome profile and stay unlocked until you click Lock now or close Chrome. To cap what any key can cost, set a spend limit in the Anthropic Console. Set a spend limit
  • Nothing goes to the developer or to any analytics service. Privacy policy

    Auto-detect

    Off unless you turn it on.

    When this is on, Job Notifier reads the visible text of pages you open, in your browser, to spot job postings and application confirmations. When a confirmation follows a posting, it asks before sending anything to Claude. Nothing is sent until you click Find friends.

    Turning it on asks Chrome for access to all sites, because applications happen on many different sites. Turning it off stops all page reading. Without it, click the Job Notifier toolbar button on a posting.

    Privacy and data

    Your API key, settings, and friends are encrypted with your passphrase in this browser and stay unlocked until you click Lock now or close Chrome. Job postings you view are kept in memory until you close their tab or Chrome.
