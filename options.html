Job Notifier settings

Everything here is stored only in this browser. Your API key and friends' details are never shared with job sites. Claude only sees the job posting and your group names, never your friends' names or emails.

Claude

Create one at console.anthropic.com.

Groups

Make a group for each kind of job you share, like Software, Cyber, Law or Health. When you apply somewhere, Claude suggests the group that fits and you can switch it with one tap.

    Friends

    Tap a group to put a friend in it. A friend can be in several groups, and a friend in no group won't be emailed.
